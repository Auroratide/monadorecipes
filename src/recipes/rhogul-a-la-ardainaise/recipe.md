# Rhogul à la Ardainaise

A light chicken stew with a variety of vegetables.

* Type: Veggie
* Source: Aegaeon's Refined Cuisine
* Prep: 15 minutes
* Time: 45 minutes
* Rarity: 1

## Ingredients

| Ingredient           | Quantity       | Rarity | Type      |
| -------------------- | -------------- | ------ | --------- |
| Chicken Breast       | 1 1/2 pounds   | 2      | Meat      |
| Yellow Onion (diced) | 1              | 1      | Vegetable |
| Garlic (minced)      | 2 teaspoons    | 1      | Vegetable |
| Carrots (chunks)     | 4              | 1      | Vegetable |
| Celery (sliced)      | 4              | 1      | Vegetable |
| Corn                 | 15 ounces      | 1      | Vegetable |
| Arugula (chopped)    | 1/4 cup        | 2      | Vegetable |
| Butter               | 3 tablespoons  | 1      | Oil       |
| Olive Oil            | 1 tablespoon   | 1      | Oil       |
| Chicken Broth        | 4 cups         | 1      | Meat      |
| Italian Seasoning    | 2 teaspoons    | 2      | Spice     |
| Cold Water           | 1/3 cups       | 1      | Liquid    |
| Cornstarch           | 3 tablespoons  | 1      | Grain     |
| Salt                 | -              | 1      | Spice     |

## Directions

1. Heat 1 tablespoon of butter and 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a pot over medium-high heat. Season the chicken with salt and then add to the pan, cooking 5 minutes per side. Afterward, remove it from the head and shred it. [Chicken Breast: 1 1/2 pounds, Butter: 1 tablespoon, Olive Oil: 1 tablespoon]
2. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter to the pot over medium-high heat. Stir in the onion, garlic, celery, and carrots. Cook for 8 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add some salt as well. [Yellow Onion (diced): 1, Garlic (minced): 2 teaspoons, Celery (sliced): 4, Carrots (chunks): 4]
3. Pour the chicken broth, and add the chicken, corn (drained), and half the chopped arugula. Add the Italian Seasoning as well. [Chicken Broth: 4 cups, Corn: 15 ounces, Arugula (chopped): 1/8 cup, Italian Seasoning: 2 teaspoons]
4. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. In a separate bowl, mix together the 1/3 cup of watter and cornstarch, until dissolved. Stir it into the stew and cook for another 10 minutes, until thickened.
6. Garnish with the remaining arugula. Serve hot with some bread! [Arugula (chopped): 1/8 cup]


## Interpretation

* Deviled Onion: 1
* Tawny Carrot: 2
* Rhogul Birdmeat: 1
* Hustle Hyacinth: 2
* Shepherd's Purse: 2

The Japanese name for this dish implies it is stewed, and so we went with a chicken stew as the base recipe. Rhoguls are bird-like creatures, and so chicken was chosen as the meat. The two interesting ingredients here are the Hustle Hyacinth and Shepherd's Purse.

Although Hyacinth is a flower normally, in the games it is a vegetable item, and is used in pies and salsa. Corn is used to represent Hustle Hyacinth, as it works in all the recipes that call for it, and corn stalks vaguely resemble hyacinth flowers.

Shepherd's Purse is an actual plant, and in fact it could be used if available to you. We chose Arugula to represent Shepherd's Purse, as it retains a similar earthy profile while being more readily available in stores.
